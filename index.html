About The Show

"In 325 A.D., Emperor Constantine assembles the early Church to define Christian orthodoxy. But holy debates spiral into unholy chaos as four unruly bishops and one confused emperor clash over the nature of Christ."

Featuring slapstick theology, original musical numbers, and a frankly irresponsible amount of wine, Oh Constantine! blends historical events with modern sensibility — examining how theological debates shaped Christianity while never losing sight of the very human drama behind the dogma.

Originally staged at the Hollywood Fringe Festival 2025 at the Zephyr Theatre, the show received an award nomination and rave reviews. It is now seeking its next stage.
